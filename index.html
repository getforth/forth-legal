Support

Email nikitaborisov724@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Forth has no accounts, so there's nothing to look up — just tell us what happened and which iPhone you're using.

Managing your subscription

Forth's subscription is billed through Apple. To view, change, or cancel it, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

If you already paid and the app isn't unlocked, tap Restore purchase on the payment screen.

The Room

You can report any post and block any handle from inside the app, and reports reach a person. If a post of yours was blocked and you think that was wrong, email us and say what you were trying to say. Posts are deleted seven days after they are written.

Your data

Your answers, progress, and blocker settings live on your phone, not on a server. You can erase everything from the Privacy screen inside the app.
