Managing your subscription

For an Apple subscription, to view, change, or cancel it, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

If you paid through Apple and the app isn't unlocked, tap Restore purchase on the payment screen.

For a web subscription, use Manage subscription in Forth's Privacy screen or the management link in your purchase email. The web offer charges US $1 for the first seven days, then US $39.99 per year automatically until cancelled. Cancel before the first seven days end to avoid the first annual charge. Access continues through the period already paid for.

To recover a web purchase after reinstalling Forth, open the access link in your purchase email on the device with Forth installed. For web billing or refund help, contact the support email above and include your purchase email address. Do not send card numbers or security codes.

The Room

You can report any post and block any handle from inside the app. Reports are available for manual review. If a post of yours was held, rejected, or removed and you think that was wrong, email us and say what you were trying to say. Posts leave the feed after seven days and are deleted from the active Room database shortly afterward, as explained in the Privacy Policy.
