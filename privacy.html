Privacy Policy

Forth · Effective August 2, 2026

The short version. Forth has no accounts and no sign-up. Your assessment answers, session history, progress, and blocker settings are stored on your phone and are never sent to us. The one exception is The Room, Forth's community feed: posts you write there are sent to our server, checked before anyone sees them, and deleted after seven days. We collect a small amount of anonymous usage data to see which parts of the app people use, and you can turn that off inside the app. We never collect your notes, your answers, the sites you block, or your browsing history.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Forth iOS app. Forth is operated by Nikita Borisov. You can reach us at nikitaborisov724@gmail.com.

No account, no sign-up

Forth does not ask you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. There is no profile to log into.

If you use The Room, your phone creates a random id for itself so the feed knows which posts are yours. It holds no name, email, or phone number, and it is not connected to your subscription. The section below explains how that works.

What stays on your device

The following is stored locally on your phone and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your assessment answers and the plan generated from them
  • Your reset session history, timings, and any intensity ratings
  • Your streak, progress, world discoveries, and saved gallery artwork
  • Your blocker settings, including any custom website addresses you add
  • Your app preferences, including reminder times and privacy settings

You can erase all of this at any time from the Privacy screen inside the app. Deleting the app also removes it. Because it never leaves your device, we cannot recover it for you, and we cannot see it.

The Room

The Room is Forth's community feed. It is the one part of the app that sends something you wrote to our server. Everything listed above stays on your phone. What you post here does not.

Posts are up to 500 characters, with replies one level deep. Every post is deleted seven days after it is written, along with its replies. Nothing you write builds into a history that someone could read back later.

Your handle

You do not choose a name. Forth generates one for you from a fixed wordlist, like QuietHeron-482. This is deliberate. People tend to reuse a username they already have somewhere else, and in this category that can identify you to anyone who recognises it.

You can change your handle whenever you want. Old posts keep the old handle so conversations still read properly, and anything you write afterwards is not connected to what came before. There is no profile page anywhere in The Room, so nobody can pull up a list of everything one handle has written. Deleting the app loses the handle for good.

Your subscription is not connected to your posts

The Room is part of the paid subscription, so our server has to confirm that you are subscribed. It does that without learning which subscriber you are.

When you subscribe, your phone makes up a random token and hides it behind a random number of its own before sending it to us. We check your purchase with Apple and sign the token while it is still hidden, so we never see its value. Your phone then removes the hiding. When you first open The Room, it hands back a token we have never laid eyes on. No record connects your Apple purchase to your handle, because that connection is never created in the first place. It is not that we promise not to look. There is nothing there to look at.

Moderation

Every post is checked automatically before anyone else can see it, and that check reads the full text of what you wrote. There is no way to moderate a feed without something reading it, and we would rather say so than let you assume your posts are unreadable.

The check runs through Anthropic's Claude API, which receives the text of the post and nothing else. No handle, no id, no subscription information. Anthropic does not use it to train their models, and neither do we. We do not keep the text of the check after it finishes.

Posts are held or blocked for sexual or explicit content, contact details or links, shaming and moralising, claims that anything cures addiction, spam, and harassment. If your post is blocked you are told which rule it hit and how to appeal. If a post suggests you may be in crisis, it is held and you are shown support resources rather than a rejection notice.

You can report any post and block any handle from inside the app. Reports reach a person.

What this does not protect you from

We would rather be straight with you about the limits than oversell this.

  • Being anonymous depends on there being a crowd to be anonymous in. Forth is new, and while the community is small, the timing of a post can narrow down who wrote it.
  • None of this hides what you actually say. A post that contains identifying details identifies you, no matter how the account behind it works.
  • Reaching The Room means your phone connects to our server, the same as visiting any website. We do not use those connections to build a picture of who you are.

Posts are never sold, never shown to advertisers, and never used to train AI models. They are not connected to your assessment answers, your progress, your blocker settings, or your subscription.

Anonymous usage analytics

Forth sends a small set of anonymous events to PostHog, an analytics provider, so we can see where people get stuck. These events record that something happened, never what you answered. For example, we record that an assessment step was completed, that a reset session started, or that a purchase screen was viewed.

These events are not linked to a personal profile, and location lookup by IP address is disabled. We do not use session recording or screen recording. Analytics never includes your answers, ratings, notes, blocked sites, web addresses, or browsing history — the app filters those out before anything is sent.

You can turn analytics off entirely on the Privacy screen inside the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and your subscription status, not your identity. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The website and app blocker

Forth's blocker uses Apple's Screen Time system. When you choose apps or websites to block, Apple gives Forth an opaque token rather than the actual names. Forth cannot read which apps or sites you selected, and neither can we. These tokens stay on your device.

Any custom website addresses you type in are stored on your device and passed only to the blocking system on your phone. They are never sent to us or to analytics. Forth does not read, monitor, or record your browsing history.

Creator codes

If you install Forth through a creator's link, the app stores a short creator code so we can credit that creator. It is a plain code, not an advertising identifier, and we do not use device fingerprinting or track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your phone. We do not operate a push notification server and cannot send you messages.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show ads or share data with advertising networks.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your notes, answers, blocked destinations, or browsing history.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not use community posts to train AI models.
  • We do not link community posts to your subscription, your plan, or your progress.

Children

Forth is intended for adults and is rated for users 17 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off analytics — Privacy screen in the app.
  • Erase your data — Privacy screen in the app, or delete the app.
  • Lock the app — optional Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint lock.

Because Forth holds no account and stores your information on your own device, there is no server-side record for us to export or delete on request. If you have a question about your data, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy
