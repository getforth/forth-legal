Forth reviewer access

For the Google Play review of Forth on Android.

  1. Install Forth and open it once.
  2. Enter the private credential supplied in the Google Play Console App access instructions.
  3. Choose Open Forth.

Use the complete 64-character credential. It is case-sensitive.

This page passes the credential to Forth through its Android link. Forth checks reviewer access online. This page does not send it to a website, save it or track your visit.
